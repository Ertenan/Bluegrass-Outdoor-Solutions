# Website photo checklist

The portfolio currently uses representative outdoor-service photography. Replace each image with Bluegrass Outdoor Solutions project photos as they become available.

## Before-and-after sets to capture

- Retaining wall and landscape steps: same wide angle before work, during base preparation, and after cleanup.
- Front landscape installation: straight-on curb view plus one close detail of the finished bed.
- Decorative rock installation: matching bed angle before removal and after edging/rock placement.
- Lawn installation or recovery: same height and direction before treatment and after establishment.
- Mulch installation: wide bed view before cleanup and after fresh mulch.
- Tree and shrub planting: matching property view before planting and after final watering/mulch.

## Photo standards

- Photograph from the same marked position and camera height for a true comparison.
- Use landscape orientation, preferably 4:3 or wider, with the main work centered.
- Shoot in daylight, avoid parked vehicles and customer-identifying details, and get permission before publishing.
- Keep original, full-resolution files; use clear names such as `retaining-wall-richmond-before.jpg` and `retaining-wall-richmond-after.jpg`.

## Future website update

When at least three strong matched sets are available, restore an optional before-and-after comparison for those real projects only. Keep ordinary project cards as single-photo cards so unmatched jobs still look complete.
